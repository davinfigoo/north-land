Frequently Asked Questions

Is it safe to travel to Pakistan?

Yes, Pakistan is a safe and welcoming country for tourists. We prioritize your safety and only operate in secure, tourist-friendly areas like Hunza, Skardu, and Kashmir.

What is included in the package?

Our packages typically include accommodation, transportation, breakfast, and guide services. Flight tickets are usually excluded unless specified otherwise.

Can appropriate for solo female travelers?

Absolutely! Many female travelers visit Northern Pakistan. Our guides are professional and respectful, ensuring a comfortable environment for everyone.

How do I book a trip?

You can easily book by clicking the "Contact Us" or WhatsApp button on the package cards. Our team will guide you through the process.
